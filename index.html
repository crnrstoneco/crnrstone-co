3D floor model

See how a floor goes together, layer by layer, and turn it around in 3D. Tap any part to learn what it is, what it sits on and its typical size.

  • Concrete slab: footings, reo, mesh and set-downs, before and after the pour
  • Timber floor: timber, concrete or steel stumps or brick piers, then bearers and joists
  • Flooring types: sheet (platform) or strip flooring, with fibre cement sheet in the wet area
  • An attached timber deck you can add or take away
  • Matching plan drawings
  • Measurements in mm for every part, shown on the model
